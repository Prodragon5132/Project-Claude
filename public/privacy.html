Privacy

Last updated 31 August 2026

SafePaste exists so that sensitive text does not get sent to a third party. It would be absurd for the tool itself to be that third party, so it is not.

The documents you paste

Your text never leaves your browser. Detection, redaction and restoration all run as JavaScript on your own machine. There is no upload, no processing queue, no copy on a server, and nothing to delete afterwards, because nothing was ever transmitted.

This is enforced, not merely promised. Every page is served with a Content Security Policy containing connect-src 'self'. Your browser will refuse to let the page open a connection to any other host. You can confirm this yourself: open developer tools, select the Network tab, paste a document, and watch nothing happen.

What is stored on your device

WhatWhereWhy
Your settings — which detectors are on, token style, custom rules, allowlist localStorage in your browser So you do not reconfigure the tool every visit
Your licence key and plan, if you bought one localStorage in your browser So Pro features stay unlocked
Your theme choice localStorage in your browser Light, dark or follow the system
A copy of the app's own files The browser's cache, via a service worker So the tool works with no network connection

The document you are working on, the findings and the mapping vault live in the tab's memory only. Closing the tab discards them. Clearing site data for this domain removes everything in the table above.

What reaches our servers

There are exactly two requests, and neither carries your content.

  • Loading the page. Like any website, this produces a normal web server request. Our host records standard access logs.
  • Activating a licence. When you paste a licence key and press Activate, that key is sent to /api/license so it can be checked with the payment provider. Nothing else is sent — not your document, not a fingerprint, not an identifier. The result is cached in your browser so the check does not repeat on every visit.

Analytics, tracking and cookies

There are none. No analytics script, no tag manager, no pixel, no session recording, no A/B testing tool, no error reporting service, no advertising network, and no cookies. The site sets no cookies at all, which is why there is no cookie banner.

Payment

If you buy a licence, the payment is handled entirely by our payment provider on their own checkout page. Your card details never touch this site and we never see them. The provider holds your name, email address and purchase record so they can issue receipts, handle refunds and validate licence keys. Their privacy policy governs that data; a link to it appears at checkout.

Data we hold about you

If you have not bought anything: nothing beyond ordinary web server logs. If you have: your email address and purchase record, held by the payment provider on our behalf, used only to deliver your licence, answer support email and process refunds. We do not sell it, share it for marketing, or add you to a mailing list you did not ask for.

Your rights

If you are in the UK, EU or another jurisdiction with equivalent law, you have the right to access, correct, export or delete the personal data we hold about you, and to object to its processing. Since the only such data is your purchase record, a request is quick to fulfil. Write to the address on your receipt.

Children

SafePaste is a business tool and is not directed at children under 16. We do not knowingly collect data about them.

Changes

If this policy changes in a way that affects you, the date at the top changes and the substance of the change is described here. The commitment that your documents stay in your browser is not something we intend to revisit; it is the entire product.
